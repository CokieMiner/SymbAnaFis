## [0.9.0] - 2026-11-10

### Major Architecture & Engine Redesign

Version 0.9.0 is a ground-up redesign of `symb_anafis` focused on high performance, immutable DAG semantics, exact rational arithmetic via `NumAnaFis`, a single canonical API, and seamless Rust and Python interoperability.
